PathSnap / Overview

Geospatial Map Matching

Reconstruct true road routes from noisy GPS trajectories.

Consumer GPS devices suffer from satellite multipath, clock jitter, and urban canyons. PathSnap combines spatial road candidate generation, supervised machine-learning candidate scoring, and topology-constrained Hidden Markov sequence optimization to project noisy GPS observations onto the true underlying road network.

How Map Matching Works

The end-to-end PathSnap pipeline explained in five understandable stages.

1

GPS Observations

Raw vehicle tracking points recorded with timestamps, noisy latitude, longitude, and speed.

Input trajectory
2

Find Possible Roads

Spatial indexing queries nearby road segments (arcs) within a 75-meter candidate search radius.

k=16 candidates per point
3

ML Candidate Scoring

Classifiers evaluate proximity distance, heading alignment, speed, and road length to score each road.

13 engineered features
4

Route Reconstruction

The HMM / Viterbi decoder enforces road network connectivity, eliminating impossible road jumps.

Topology transition penalties
5

Matched Route

A seamless, connected vehicle journey aligned with the actual street geometry and junctions.

Final verified path

Key Capabilities

Engineered for robust spatial reasoning and viva evaluation.

📐

Local Projected Geometry

Strict UTM 32N / EPSG:25832 metric projection ensures distance and spatial gap calculations avoid spherical distortion.

⚖️

Fair Trip-Grouped Folds

Cross-validation strictly uses 5-fold GroupKFold assignments across the 80 training trips, preventing observation leakage.

⚡

High-Speed Parquet Pipeline

Precomputed columnar candidate stores allow sub-2-second inference and sequence decoding across thousands of GPS points.

🛡️

Air-Gapped Ground Truth

Reference routes remain server-side for offline evaluation. Client APIs receive strictly predicted geometry and telemetry.
